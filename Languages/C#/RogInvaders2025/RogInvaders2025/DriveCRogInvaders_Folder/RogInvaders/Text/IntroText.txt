In the beginning all was well, birds sang, bunnies hopped, trees rustled softly in a gentle breeze.
Everything was fine. Or was it? Out in the dark vastness of the cosmos, entities alien in origin had
a plan. An evil plan. A plan so hideous that its form and conception made even Satan himself gasp
in awe at such unfathomable evil. Thus the aliens set their plan into action and sent attack forces to
the Earth to not just disable the weapons of mankind and subject him to their will but to INVADE!!!
Only one brave pilot stands before the indomitable invading force - YOU! Can you stop the attack?
Using your spaceship created by top boffins in white coats equipped with the newly developed ray
gun the NuPhonic ray you can destroy the invaders and save mankind and all the fluffy creatures
of Earth, we are all counting on you commander. Good luck, you'll need it!
